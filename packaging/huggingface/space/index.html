Phonlet Wake · en-1 · 1 MB · offline

Any wake word. Typed, not trained.

Type a phrase. The generator calibrates it here in your browser, then the engine listens for it on your microphone. Nothing is uploaded.

Two to four syllables work best. Names and made-up words get a predicted pronunciation; write the sounds yourself as zorblax=Z AO R B L AE K S.

    Aims at about 1 false alarm per hour of speech.
